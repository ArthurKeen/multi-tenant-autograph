Project Tidewatch — Residential Demand-Response Program
(FICTIONAL TEST DATA — Tenant B)

Summary
Project Tidewatch is Solara Energy Retail's flagship residential demand-response program in
the Sunbelt Metro territory. It enrolls customers on the FlexPeak tariff into automated
load-shifting events and rewards participation through EcoReward points. The program is led
by Program Manager Marcus Okafor.

Goal
Reduce SER's contracted peak purchases during the Sunbelt Metro summer system peak by a
fictional target of 75 MW, lowering wholesale costs and passing savings back to customers.

How it works
1. Customers on the FlexPeak tariff opt in to Project Tidewatch and connect a smart
   thermostat or smart water heater.
2. On high-price days, SER dispatches a Tidewatch event: connected devices pre-cool or shift
   load before the Sunbelt Metro peak window.
3. Verified reductions earn EcoReward points, and customers can opt out of any single event
   without losing their tariff benefits.

Enrollment tiers
- Tidewatch Basic: thermostat-only participation.
- Tidewatch Plus: thermostat plus water heater, higher EcoReward multipliers.
- Tidewatch Community: multi-family buildings in Sunbelt Metro Central enrolled in bulk.

Results to date
In its first fictional summer, Project Tidewatch enrolled 48,000 homes and delivered an
average event reduction of 61 MW, approaching the 75 MW target. EcoReward redemptions rose
sharply, improving customer retention on the FlexPeak tariff.

Roadmap
- Add behavioral nudges through the SER mobile app.
- Extend Project Tidewatch to small-commercial accounts in Sunbelt Metro West.
- Integrate rooftop-solar export signals into event dispatch.

All details are fictional and used only for isolation testing.
