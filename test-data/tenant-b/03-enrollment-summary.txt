FlexPeak & EcoReward — Quarterly Enrollment Summary
(FICTIONAL TEST DATA — Tenant B)

Prepared by: Solara Energy Retail, Demand-Side Coordination
Author: Marcus Okafor, Program Manager

1. Retail enrollment
SER closed the quarter with a fictional 320,000 active accounts across Sunbelt Metro. Net
adds were strongest in Sunbelt Metro South, driven by summer air-conditioning customers
seeking the FlexPeak tariff.

2. FlexPeak tariff
FlexPeak enrollment reached 128,000 accounts (40 percent of the book). Customers on FlexPeak
shifted an average of 14 percent of their peak-window usage, reducing SER's exposure to
wholesale peak prices.

3. EcoReward program
EcoReward issued points to 110,000 households this quarter. Redemptions were concentrated in
bill credits. The highest point earners were Project Tidewatch Plus participants who shifted
load during dispatched events.

4. Project Tidewatch
Project Tidewatch grew to 48,000 enrolled homes. Event participation averaged 88 percent,
and opt-outs stayed below the 5 percent threshold that triggers a program review. The team
is preparing the Sunbelt Metro West small-commercial expansion.

5. Customer satisfaction
Survey scores were highest among EcoReward redeemers and Tidewatch participants, reinforcing
the link between demand-side engagement and retention on the FlexPeak tariff.

All metrics, programs, and names are fictional and exist solely to exercise multi-tenant
data isolation in the GraphRAG prototype.
