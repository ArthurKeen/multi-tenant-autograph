Solara Energy Retail — Organizational Overview
(FICTIONAL TEST DATA — Tenant B)

Solara Energy Retail (SER) is a fictional competitive electricity retailer serving
residential and small-commercial customers across the Sunbelt Metro service territory.
SER buys wholesale power and sells retail plans; it does not own transmission lines or
generation assets. Its competitive edge is demand-side coordination — helping customers
shift and reduce usage during expensive peak periods.

Mission
SER's mission is to give Sunbelt Metro customers lower bills and a smaller footprint through
smart tariffs, connected devices, and rewards for flexible energy use.

Service territory
- Sunbelt Metro Central: high-density apartments and small businesses.
- Sunbelt Metro South: suburban single-family homes with high air-conditioning load.
- Sunbelt Metro West: mixed residential with growing rooftop solar adoption.

Core products
- FlexPeak tariff: a time-of-use retail plan that rewards customers for moving usage away
  from the Sunbelt Metro summer peak.
- EcoReward: a loyalty program that grants points for verified energy reductions, redeemable
  against future bills.
- Project Tidewatch: SER's flagship residential demand-response program (see dedicated brief).

Leadership
The demand-side coordination group is led by Program Manager Marcus Okafor, who owns the
FlexPeak tariff design, the EcoReward incentive engine, and the Project Tidewatch rollout.

Customer base
SER serves a fictional 320,000 retail accounts across Sunbelt Metro. Roughly 40 percent are
enrolled in the FlexPeak tariff, and EcoReward has issued points to over 110,000 households.

All names, programs, and figures in this document are fictional and exist only to test
multi-tenant data isolation.
