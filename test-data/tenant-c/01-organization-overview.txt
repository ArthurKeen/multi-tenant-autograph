Verdant Power Developments — Organizational Overview
(FICTIONAL TEST DATA — Tenant C)

Verdant Power Developments (VPD) is a fictional renewable energy developer that originates,
builds, and finances utility-scale solar and wind projects. VPD does not operate a
transmission grid or sell retail electricity; it develops generation assets and sells their
output under long-term power purchase agreements.

Mission
VPD's mission is to bring low-cost renewable generation online quickly by managing the full
development lifecycle: site control, interconnection, permitting, financing, and
construction handover.

Development pipeline
- Project Helios Fields: a utility-scale solar farm at the Dunesong site (flagship project).
- Windward Bluffs: an onshore wind project on a coastal ridge.
- Several earlier-stage prospects under land option.

Key sites
- Dunesong: a high-irradiance desert plateau hosting Project Helios Fields.
- Windward Bluffs: a consistently windy coastal ridgeline.

Commercial approach
VPD sells output through the Greenline PPA framework — its standardized long-term offtake
contract template used to de-risk financing. Greenline PPAs are structured for creditworthy
offtakers seeking fixed-price renewable energy.

Leadership
The development group is led by Head of Development Priya Raman, who owns site origination,
the interconnection strategy, and Greenline PPA negotiations across the pipeline.

Stage gates
VPD advances projects through five internal gates: site control, interconnection feasibility,
permitting, financing close, and notice to proceed. Project Helios Fields is the most
advanced asset in the pipeline.

All names, sites, projects, and figures in this document are fictional and exist only to
test multi-tenant data isolation.
