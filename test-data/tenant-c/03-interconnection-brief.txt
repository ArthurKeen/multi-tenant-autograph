1. Pipeline overview
This brief covers the interconnection status of VPD's two lead projects: Project Helios
Fields at the Dunesong site and the Windward Bluffs wind project on the coastal ridge.

2. Project Helios Fields (Dunesong)
The Dunesong interconnection feasibility study confirmed deliverability with only minor
network upgrades. Project Helios Fields holds a queue position consistent with its target
financing close, and the co-located battery improves its interconnection efficiency.

3. Windward Bluffs (wind)
Windward Bluffs is a fictional 300 MW onshore wind project on a consistently windy coastal
ridgeline. Its interconnection feasibility study is underway. Wake modeling across the ridge
informs turbine layout, and VPD is evaluating whether to pair the project with storage like
the Dunesong design.

4. Commercial path
Both projects are being marketed under the Greenline PPA framework. Project Helios Fields is
already committed under a 20-year Greenline PPA; Windward Bluffs offtake is in negotiation
with prospective corporate buyers.

5. Next steps
- Close financing on Project Helios Fields and issue notice to proceed at Dunesong.
- Advance the Windward Bluffs interconnection study and finalize turbine selection.
- Standardize the Greenline PPA terms across the remaining pipeline prospects.

All sites, projects, names, and figures are fictional and exist solely to exercise
multi-tenant data isolation in the GraphRAG prototype.
