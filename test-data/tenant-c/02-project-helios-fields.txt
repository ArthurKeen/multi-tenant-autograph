Project Helios Fields — Utility-Scale Solar Development
(FICTIONAL TEST DATA — Tenant C)

Summary
Project Helios Fields is Verdant Power Developments' flagship utility-scale solar farm,
sited on the Dunesong plateau. The project is led by Head of Development Priya Raman and is
contracted under a Greenline PPA.

Project specifications
- Nameplate capacity: a fictional 450 MW of single-axis tracking photovoltaic arrays.
- Location: the Dunesong site, chosen for high solar irradiance and gentle terrain.
- Storage: a co-located 120 MW / 480 MWh battery to firm output and shift evening delivery.

Development status
Project Helios Fields has cleared site control, interconnection feasibility, and permitting,
and is approaching financing close. The Dunesong interconnection study identified only minor
network upgrades, keeping the project on its target schedule.

Offtake
The full output of Project Helios Fields is committed under a 20-year Greenline PPA to a
creditworthy corporate offtaker seeking fixed-price renewable supply. The Greenline structure
allowed VPD to secure favorable construction financing terms.

Construction plan
- Mobilization and civil works at the Dunesong site.
- Tracker and module installation across the plateau in sequenced blocks.
- Battery integration and commissioning.
- Handover to a long-term operations contractor.

Risks
- Supply-chain timing for trackers and battery cells.
- Heat and dust mitigation during Dunesong summer construction.
- Coordination of the storage dispatch profile with the Greenline PPA delivery terms.

Expected outcome
Once energized, Project Helios Fields is expected to be VPD's largest operating asset and the
template for replicating the Dunesong-plus-storage model at future sites. All details are
fictional and used only for isolation testing.
