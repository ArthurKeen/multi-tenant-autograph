Cascadia North Quarterly Reliability Report
(FICTIONAL TEST DATA — Tenant A)

Prepared by: Northwind Grid Authority, Harbormere Control Center
Author: Dana Holloway, Chief Reliability Engineer

1. System conditions
The Cascadia North grid met all firm load this quarter with no involuntary load shedding.
Peak demand reached a fictional 18,900 MW during a cold snap in the Core region, served
largely by Highlands hydro delivered over the Aurora Interconnect.

2. Transmission performance
- Aurora Interconnect availability: 99.7 percent. One planned outage was coordinated for
  Project Ironclad Phase 2 protection commissioning.
- Mistral Substation: operated within ratings throughout the quarter. Phase 1 of Project
  Ironclad reduced relay misoperations at the station to zero.

3. Interconnection queue
NGA processed 14 new interconnection requests, dominated by coastal wind imports along the
Cascadia North Coast corridor. None required network upgrades beyond existing plans.

4. Notable events
- A lightning storm in the Highlands caused a momentary trip on one Aurora Interconnect
  circuit; the redundant circuit held and no load was lost. This event reinforced the
  rationale for the Project Ironclad redundancy upgrades at the interconnect terminals.

5. Outlook
NGA expects tight reserves during the upcoming Highlands maintenance season and will
sequence remaining Project Ironclad outages at Mistral Substation accordingly.

All metrics, place names, and events in this report are fictional and exist solely to
exercise multi-tenant data isolation in the GraphRAG prototype.
